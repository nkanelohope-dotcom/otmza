About On The Move ZA

Driven by Purpose. Defined by Excellence.

Who We Are

ON THE MOVE ZA is a professional cleaning and property support company dedicated to delivering dependable, high-quality solutions for residential, commercial and corporate clients.

We understand that clean, well-maintained spaces contribute to healthier environments, improved productivity and a positive first impression. Our approach combines professionalism, attention to detail and consistent service delivery.

From routine maintenance cleaning to specialised deep cleaning, move-in/move-out services, post-construction cleaning and office cleaning, we tailor our services to meet the unique requirements of each client.

As we continue to grow, our vision is to become a trusted leader in South Africa’s cleaning industry while creating meaningful employment opportunities and making a positive impact in the communities we serve.

Our Vision

Trusted Partner

To become South Africa’s most trusted property transition partner, setting the standard for excellence in cleaning, property preparation and relocation services.

Industry Transformation

To transform the property support industry through innovation, exceptional service and strategic partnerships with estate agencies across South Africa.

To create clean, healthy environments while uplifting communities through youth employment, training and meaningful career opportunities.
