A Vision Built on Purpose

JADE is an ambitious entrepreneur, visionary leader, and the founder of Wakhile NN Holdings (Pty) Ltd. Her entrepreneurial journey has been shaped by resilience, determination, and a deep passion for building businesses that make a positive impact.

Driven by a commitment to excellence, JADE established Wakhile NN Holdings with the vision of creating a company that delivers premium services while creating opportunities for growth, employment, and community development.

Her hands-on leadership style, strong work ethic, and attention to detail have enabled the company to earn the trust of its clients through professionalism and consistent service delivery.

Under her leadership, the company continues to expand its service offerings — including On The Move ZA (professional cleaning, packing and moving) — embracing innovation and maintaining high standards across every project.

“True success is measured not only by business growth but by the positive difference a company makes in the lives of its clients, employees, and the communities it serves.” — JADE Boikhutso, Founder & Managing Director
